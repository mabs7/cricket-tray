Team & Global Scope

My Team or International

Follow your national side (+ its T20/Hundred league) or switch to senior internationals & 6 leagues (PSL, IPL, BPL, BBL, SA20, The Hundred).

Match Center

Pin One Match, Focus-Safe

Browse fixtures with competition filter pills, pin any match to the Live Strip or cycle with ◀ / ▶—zero focus theft on Windows.

Break Detection

Stumps, Lunch & Rain Delay

Displays STUMPS, LUNCH, TEA, INNINGS BREAK, RAIN DELAY & BAD LIGHT instead of false LIVE, with chronological Test innings & declarations (434/9d).

Smart Polling

Adaptive Refresh Cadence

Refreshes every 45s live, 2m during breaks/delays, 5m at Stumps or match day, and 30m when idle.

Self-Updating

SHA-256 Portable Updater

One-click Windows updates with digest verification, backup, stale update cleanup, and automatic rollback.

Resilient & Private

Stale-Safe, Zero Telemetry

Retains the last reliable score if Cricbuzz fails, requires no API keys, and saves your team and layout locally.

Quick Answers.

First-run security prompts, feed modes, and privacy.

Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper?

Releases are unsigned so the widget stays free. On Windows, click More info → Run anyway. On macOS, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and allow PakCricket.app.

How do I switch teams or leagues?

Open Match Center on Windows (or the menu bar dropdown on macOS) anytime to pick any of the 12 supported teams or switch to International & 6 Major Leagues.

How do updates and settings work?

Preferences live locally in settings.json. Windows verifies release ZIPs via SHA-256 with automatic rollback if a new build fails to start.
